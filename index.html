How it works, on both sides

For workers

No fees. You never pay us for a job.

  1. 3 min

    Apply from your phone

    Name, phone, messenger and city. No CV needed.

  2. 1 day

    A recruiter calls you

    In Ukrainian, Polish, Russian or English, on Viber, WhatsApp or Telegram.

  3. 2–5 days

    Documents and contract

    We check your papers and help with PESEL and the work permit if needed.

  4. Day 1

    Room, transport, first shift

    Keys on arrival, a bus to the plant and a coordinator on site.
