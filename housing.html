Where you can live

What the rent covers

  • A bed, bedding and a locker with a key
  • Electricity, heating, water and Wi-Fi
  • A shared kitchen with cookware
  • A laundry room or washing machine
  • Cleaning of shared areas twice a week
  • A bus to the plant where the job says so

House rules

  • Quiet hours 22:00–06:00, and quiet in the day too: night-shift neighbours sleep.
  • No alcohol in shared areas, no smoking indoors.
  • Guests until 21:00, agreed with the coordinator.
  • Leave the kitchen as you found it.
  • Give 7 days’ notice before moving out.
  • Rent is deducted from pay; the first month is charged by the day.
