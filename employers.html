Food production staff on your line in 48 hours

Temporary and permanent crews for meat, dairy, bakery, cold-storage and packing plants in central Poland. Legal, insured and coordinated on site.

Start a requestCall +48 000 000 000

What we take off your hands

  • Line crews

    From 2 to 60 people per shift for production and packing lines.

  • Seasonal peaks

    Christmas, Easter and harvest crews booked weeks ahead and released when the peak ends.

  • Cold-storage teams

    Pickers, loaders and reach-truck operators with medical checks for freezer work.

  • On-site coordinator

    One person who speaks Polish and the crew’s language and handles absences and replacements.

  • Housing and transport

    Checked rooms and shift buses, so your crew arrives rested and on time.
