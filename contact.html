Questions and answers

For workers

Do I pay anything to get a job?

No. Polish law forbids agencies from charging workers, and we never do. Rent is the only deduction, and only if you choose our housing.

Can I work without speaking Polish?

Yes, in most line jobs. Many team leaders speak Ukrainian or Russian, and every job shows the languages spoken at work.

Which documents do I need?

A passport and the right to work in Poland: a visa, a residence card or another permit. We help arrange the work permit and the PESEL number.

When and how am I paid?

Monthly, by the 10th, to a Polish bank account. You can get an advance of up to 500 PLN after your first week.

What happens on my first day?

A coordinator meets you, hands over the room keys and workwear, and takes you to the plant for safety training.

For employers

How fast can you start?

Up to 15 people in Łódź or Poznań, typically within 48 hours. For larger crews we send a written plan within 4 hours.

Who employs the workers?

Mostova is the employer under temporary work contracts. You get one monthly invoice per site.

What if someone does not show up?

The on-site coordinator arranges a replacement, usually within one shift.

Do you cover sites outside Łódź and Poznań?

Yes, across central Poland. Housing and transport are planned for each new site.

Staff for food production in Łódź, Poznań and central Poland.
